🔢 SMCS Code Reference
PARSING GUIDE
FORMAT SMCS
XXXX-YYY-Z
XXXX = 4 digit SMCS Code
YYY = 3 digit Job Code
Z = Modifier (huruf)
CONTOH PARSING
1250-045-L
SMCS: 1250 (Engine)
Job Code: 045
Modifier: L (Left)
ATURAN PARSING
Job Desk: tanpa arah (no LEFT/RIGHT)
Replacement %: > 0% only
Source: 14 kolom standar
📖 SOP & Prosedur
STANDAR PT CDE
SOP-MES-01
Prosedur Inspeksi Harian Unit
Langkah-langkah pelaksanaan inspeksi harian — pre-shift, post-shift, defect reporting, dan dokumentasi.
SOP-MES-02
Prosedur Preventive Maintenance (PM)
Alur pelaksanaan PM terjadwal — WO generation, persiapan material, execution, dan sign-off.
SOP-MES-03
Prosedur Pengambilan Sampel SOS
Teknik pengambilan sampel oli/coolant — waktu optimal, kontainer, label, dan pengiriman sampel ke lab.
SOP-MES-04
Alur Order Spare Part (MES)
WO → cek stok → PR/CP → sourcing & bidding → approval PO → penerimaan → supply → GR → close WO.
SOP-MES-05
Prosedur Breakdown Handling
Alur penanganan breakdown — rapid response, initial diagnosis, isolasi unit, dan eskalasi ke tim teknis.
SOP-MES-06
Manajemen Komponen Routable
Proses tracking life komponen routable — registration, monitoring jam, prediksi penggantian, dan rebuild.
SOP-MES-07
Prosedur Fueling & Fuel Management
Prosedur pengisian bahan bakar unit — recording, cross-check dengan fuel meter, dan laporan konsumsi.
SOP-MES-08
Prosedur Klaim Warranty
Identifikasi komponen dalam masa warranty — proses klaim, dokumentasi, follow-up vendor, dan pelaporan.
SOP-MES-09
Prosedur Condition Monitoring 5 Elemen
Penilaian CM berdasarkan 5 elemen: SOS Rating, Inspection Rating, Site Condition, Electronic Data, Repair History.
SOP-MES-10
Investigasi Major Failure
Langkah investigasi kegagalan besar — root cause analysis, 5-why, dokumentasi, dan laporan RCFA.
SOP-MES-11
Inspeksi & Pengukuran Undercarriage
Metode pengukuran CTS UC — track sag, bushing wear, sprocket wear, dan rekomendasi tindakan.
SOP-MES-12
Analisa & Penggantian GET
Prosedur analisa Ground Engaging Tools — wear index, penentuan waktu ganti, dan type selection.
